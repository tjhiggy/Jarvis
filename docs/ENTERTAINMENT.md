# Community games and entertainment

v1.5 establishes a bounded catalog contract for daily challenges, community
challenges, predictions, memorable quotes, quote of the day, random throwbacks,
roasts, and memes. Entries are allowlisted, length-bounded, and independently
enabled. Future Discord workflows must add opt-in, rate limits, safe mention
handling, moderation controls, retention, and deletion tests before public
delivery. The catalog does not generate or publish content by itself.
