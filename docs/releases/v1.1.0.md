# Jarvis v1.1.0 Community Expansion

This release ships the persistent teams and bounded MuthaShip Coin ledger foundations. Teams are server-scoped, membership is bounded, names are duplicate-safe, and no role or channel mutation is performed. The ledger is server-scoped, idempotent, opt-out aware, and stored durably in SQLite.

Trading, ambassador cards, moderation automation, tournaments, temporary voice channels, webhooks, and external REST integrations are deferred to v1.2.
