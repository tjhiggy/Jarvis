# Jarvis v1.1.0 Community Expansion

## Shipped

- Persistent teams: server-scoped team creation, membership, bounded size, duplicate-safe names, and owner deletion. No role or channel mutation.
- MuthaShip Coin ledger foundation: server-scoped idempotent awards, bounded amounts, opt-out-aware service, and durable SQLite storage.

## Deferred

Trading, ambassador cards, moderation automation, tournaments, temporary voice channels, webhooks, and external REST integrations are tracked in v1.2. They are not part of this release.

## Verification

Each slice passed focused tests, TypeScript build, and CI verification. The v1.1 milestone is closed with two shipped issues and zero open issues.
